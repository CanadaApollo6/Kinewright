## Behavior, integration and limits

C6 implements the local S2-R App surface over the existing C4/C5 owner. A ProjectSession retains that same owner across focus, panels, finite background jobs, save/recovery and rescue. Explicit Studio activation checks raw admission and claims the writer before session/store creation. It stops the legacy recorder and suspends sidecar writes for this editing context.

The App prepares the new actor subscription and snapshot before selection publication or rescue destination mutation/sealing. Installation after handoff consists of moving that prepared view. Modal controls and producer guards cover the finite operation and pending choices. The old actor remains frozen or sealed under the existing owner rules.

Recovery uses the same selected pin, ordinary preliminary Save and ordinary legal floor. Destination changes refuse while prepared and repeat availability/explicit-abandonment text. Rescue keeps trim and unavailable-recovery omission independently explicit. Branch controls list actual owner candidates and session/base blockers with Save/Close routes. Late reconciliation reports destination proof without changing the active view; only a proven never-active completed capture can be explicitly released. Source A cannot be offered as a completed capture copy.
