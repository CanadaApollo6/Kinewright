## Behavior and contracts

`Ja1EditingSession` now composes C4: pin the complete verified target, freeze O, perform an ordinary
dirty Save with its normal earliest floor, then publish one prepared selection. The same pin
survives Save, rotation, quota refusal, retirement and retry. Existing targets require current
membership and their original expected head; retirement cannot offer or remove them. Save
As/open-project refuse while prepared. Abandonment, Close and rescue handoff disclose session-only
target availability. Duplicate completed replies reuse R.

A1 preview first selects the full explicit content/recovery graph, tries unavailable-only omission
without trim, then uses Core legal floors with independent trim/omission consent. R_ack has its own
edge outside exactly two predecessors. Required inputs, Redo, gestures and independent evidence
remain. Capacity and copied-record profile checks precede destination mutation. The actual first
lock/mkdir adopts the source and consumes slots. Every new root reaches the existing shared
fresh-open admission guard.

Immediate rescue creates a fresh B ProjectId and actor, seals manual A and abandons R. Failed,
refused, cancelled and unknown rescue preserve a previously frozen O. Unknown capture copies retain
actual owners; late A/copy proof cannot activate them or change B. A newer unknown Save on active B
is reconciled as that new attempt. Context Close fences all actors and ends source reuse without
inventing a destination outcome.

Durable agent/specialist creation and reopen allocate private actors inside the owner and register
before returning handles. Caller drop retains the actual Core/base/pins. Save alone cannot make a
live session eligible for retirement. Persist stages the complete proposal and bounded checkpoint in
one existing root transaction. Charged canonical cuts preserve floored prefixes and accepted
suffixes across unknown settlement and stale guard clones. Guards keep their original namespace
through manual rescue; only that original live actor can settle its own ticket. Close persists,
fences and seals before unregistering.
